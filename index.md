## What is AMS?

Alliance Management System (AMS) is an alliance management tool for Whiteout Survival, built around one principle: **AMS informs, humans make decisions.**

AMS tracks members, bans, power, and other details while each alliance keeps its autonomy. Every system belongs to the alliance that set it up. AMS does not spy on other alliances or look at their bans, private information, or anything else they enter. Just because information is publicly available doesn't mean AMS needs to track it.

Future editions may let separate AMS systems connect. If that happens, each alliance will still choose what to share and keep complete control of its own data.

AMS was built by an alliance leader, for alliances. Data privacy is my highest concern. Systems like this are easily built with too much overreach, and I intentionally designed this one with none.

If you have any questions or want to suggest new features, I've included multiple ways to reach me in [Contact Me](contact.md); discord is the best way if you want to request new features or have simple questions. All other inquiries should go through email.
