### Bug Reports

You can also report bugs through the official AMS bug-report form:

**[AMS Bug Report Form](https://forms.google.com/)**

When reporting a bug, please include:

* What you were doing when the problem occurred
* What you expected to happen
* What actually happened
* Any relevant error messages or screenshots

AMS does not collect telemetry or automatically transmit diagnostic information. Bug reports are submitted voluntarily.
